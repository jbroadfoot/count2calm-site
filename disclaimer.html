Talk to your doctor first

We strongly recommend speaking with a qualified healthcare provider before beginning any breathing practice if you have, or suspect you may have, any of the following conditions:

  • Asthma or COPD
  • Cardiovascular disease
  • Heart conditions
  • Epilepsy or seizure disorders
  • Anxiety disorders or PTSD
  • Neurological conditions
  • Pregnancy
  • Any undiagnosed health concern

Breathwork carries real physical risk

Controlled breathing exercises can directly affect your heart rate, blood pressure, blood oxygen levels, and autonomic nervous system. Some techniques may produce lightheadedness, tingling, emotional responses, or other physiological effects, particularly in people with underlying health conditions.

These effects are not always predictable. Use all exercises with care and at your own pace.

Stop immediately and seek medical attention if you experience chest pain, difficulty breathing, fainting, or any other concerning symptoms during or after an exercise.
